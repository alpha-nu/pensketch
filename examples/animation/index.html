Five diagrams, drawn from data · rendered by pensketch

A leaf is green because green is the one colour it refuses.

Chlorophyll eats violet-blue light at about 430 nm and deep red at about 662 nm. The middle of the spectrum it barely touches — so green light bounces off and reaches your eye. Every leaf you have ever seen is showing you its waste. What it keeps, it spends on the machinery below.

01 · overview

The whole trade, in one line

A leaf takes light, air and water, and gives back sugar and the oxygen you are breathing right now. Everything after this panel is detail.

02 · zoom

Zoom in until you find the machine

The reaction does not happen in the leaf. It happens in an organelle inside one cell of it, and that organelle has two rooms that do two different jobs.

03 · light

The light reactions: a waterfall that drives a turbine

Light knocks an electron out of Photosystem II. To replace it, the complex tears a water molecule apart — which is where every oxygen atom in the air came from. The electron then falls downhill through a chain, and the energy it sheds pumps protons upstairs. They come back down through ATP synthase, and that spinning is the ATP.

04 · calvin

The Calvin cycle: bolting carbon onto a trap

No light here at all — this runs on the ATP and NADPH the first half made. RuBisCO, the most abundant protein on Earth, clamps one CO₂ onto a five-carbon trap. Most of what comes out is spent rebuilding the trap. Six turns are needed for a single glucose.

05 · loop

Why it is one process and not two

The halves are each other's supply chain. Light reactions charge the carriers; the Calvin cycle spends them and hands the empties straight back. Stop the light and the cycle runs until the carriers are flat — which is why "dark reactions" is a misleading old name.

None of these diagrams is a picture. Each is a plain object — boxes with coordinates, edges naming the sides they attach to — and pensketch drew it in your browser just now. The panels were composed against the pensketch MCP server, the same tool an agent calls, and check_diagram passed all five with no findings.

The animation is not a video, and this page does not write it. draw is asked for order, which stamps each element with how far through the drawing it is, and @pensketch/animation puts the stylesheet that reads it inside the drawing. A solid stroke is dashed with a single dash as long as itself and slid home; a dotted one fades in instead, because normalising it the same way would render it solid.
